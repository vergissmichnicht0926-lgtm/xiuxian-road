■ CG-1「争辩」—— 主视角，被一群公司高管围住施压
Japanese 2D anime style, cel-shading, clean lineart, soft pastel color palette, galgame visual novel art style. First-person point of view. You are seated at a long dark conference table, surrounded by several tall corporate executives in black suits standing around you, looming over the table. They are arguing intensely, some pointing fingers at you, some holding up documents. Their faces are partially in shadow, stern and accusing anime expressions. Your own hands are visible in the foreground, resting on the table, fingers slightly clenched. The table surface reflects harsh fluorescent office light. Scattered papers and a half-open folder in front of you. Overhead cold white office lighting, hard shadows, tense oppressive atmosphere, muted grey and dark blue color palette with a single warm desk lamp glow in the background. Anime background art, high quality anime illustration, 16:9

■ CG-2「放弃」—— 主视角低头，一份协议推到你面前，手悬停
Japanese 2D anime style, cel-shading, clean lineart, soft pastel color palette, galgame visual novel art style. First-person point of view. Same dark conference table. A corporate hand in a black suit pushes a formal agreement document toward you across the table; the paper has dense unreadable text and a signature line at the bottom. Your own hand hovers just above the signature line, fingers slightly trembling, not yet touching the pen. The executives' hands and the edges of their sleeves frame the corners of the image. Cold harsh overhead light, long shadows, the document is the brightest thing in frame. Desaturated cold tones, heavy defeated mood, silence and pressure. Anime background art, high quality anime illustration, shallow depth of field on the document, 16:9

■ CG-3「报纸与照片」—— 深夜桌面：报纸 UCRB 实验失败，报纸下露出白发女孩的照片，男主的手扶着报纸一角
Japanese 2D anime style, cel-shading, clean lineart, soft pastel color palette, galgame visual novel art style. A single coherent scene, first-person point of view. An old wooden office desk at night, lit by a dim warm desk lamp from the left. A newspaper spread open across the desk; the masthead at the top clearly reads "UCRB" in bold letters, below it a large headline implying a failed experiment (headline text slightly blurred, a faded photo accompanying the article). At one corner, the newspaper is slightly lifted by your own hand in the foreground, revealing a small worn photograph hidden underneath — a young girl with very long flowing white-silver hair, pale skin, large pale blue-grey anime eyes, a gentle calm almost melancholic expression, the photo aged with small creases. The warm lamp light falls softly on the photo, making it glow against the cold desk, while your hand resting on the newspaper edge subtly shows a determined, weary grip. A chipped coffee cup with cold coffee at the corner, scattered pens. Melancholic, nostalgic, resolute late-night mood, warm light on cold scene. Anime background art, highly detailed, soft depth of field, 16:9

■ CG-4「颠倒的数字流」—— 潜入意识海，数字与错误警告一切颠倒
Japanese 2D anime style, cel-shading, clean lineart, soft pastel color palette, galgame visual novel art style. Surreal scene. A vast dark digital void filled with cascading streams of numbers, code fragments, and error warning symbols — but everything is inverted: the code streams fall upward instead of down, the horizon line is flipped, gravity seems reversed. The numbers and warnings are glitchy and unreadable, flickering in cyan and deep blue. In the far distance, the faint silhouette of the white-silver haired girl floats upside down, hair drifting in reverse, glowing faintly. The whole scene feels like falling into a digital sea, dizzying, dreamlike. Glitch effects, cool cyan-blue palette with sparse warm highlights, sense of free fall. Anime background art, highly detailed, surreal, 16:9
